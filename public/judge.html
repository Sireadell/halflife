What A Judge Should See In Under 60 Seconds

  • The app is alive, because the status check returns from Halflife itself.
  • The Arc proof is not just a link, because Halflife verifies the records through the app.
  • The paid path is usable, because a judge can connect a wallet, pay on Arc, and trigger a fresh run.
  • The certificate proof is open for inspection, including the saved paid proof when one exists.
